Interview Tip
After completing each day's challenge, ask yourself these questions:
	• Why did Angular provide this feature? 
	• What problem does it solve? 
	• What are the common mistakes developers make? 
	• Where have I used this in a real project? 
	• Can I explain it in under 2 minutes? 
Being able to build it, debug it, and explain it is what interviewers are usually looking for.
